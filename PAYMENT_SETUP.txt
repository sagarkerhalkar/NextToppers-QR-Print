PAYMENT MODES

A) YOUR EXISTING UPI / PAYMENT QR
- Admin -> Payment -> Upload / Replace QR.
- Customer sees the QR after submitting a print job.
- Customer pays and clicks "I have paid".
- IMPORTANT: a normal/static QR image cannot report bank payment success to this application.
- Host must verify receipt and click Approve & Print.

B) RAZORPAY AUTO PAYMENT + AUTO PRINT
- Admin -> Payment.
- Enter Razorpay Key ID + Key Secret.
- Enable Razorpay and choose "Razorpay automatic payment OR host approval".
- Every payment creates a Razorpay Order on the server.
- Browser success is signature-verified on the server.
- The server then calls Razorpay to confirm/capture the payment.
- Only a CAPTURED payment is marked paid and sent automatically to the admin-selected printer.
- For reliable webhooks in production, expose the webhook endpoint through a trusted public HTTPS URL.

Never put Key Secret in browser code or share it with customers.
